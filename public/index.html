About THIA

The Tiny Home Industry Association is a 501(c)(6) trade organization for the tiny home industry. Since 2016, we have worked to:

  • Advocate for changes to regulations
  • Develop industry best practices and construction standards
  • Make tiny homes a permitted, permanent form of housing
